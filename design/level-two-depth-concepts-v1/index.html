Level 2: depth hazards and giant-head concepts

Five proposals created October 26, 2026. These are concept illustrations, not implemented encounters or gameplay captures.

The head can dominate the screen while its dangerous bite occupies one clearly warned lane. Track damage leaves a visible persistent pit. Names, defeat mechanics, direct-hit consequences and optional hazard selection remain open.

Current status, October 26, 2026: This gallery preserves the original proposal wording. The Maintenance Hand was implemented in Prototype 110; ceiling strainers arrived in Prototype 112 and were revised in Prototype 114. The boss and wall ram remain proposals. See the Maintenance Hand record, strainer and water art record, and current build delivery.

Background Hunger

A huge head emerges from the wall shadows, commits to one lane, then lunges over intact track. Direct-hit consequences remain undecided.

Track-Eating Bite

Updated game-style head with exaggerated opening jaw. An early bite removes track ahead, leaving a persistent pit and waterfalls. The opposite bank remains open; a lane and edge warning must persist when the new hole is offscreen.

Maintenance Hand

Optional giant hand reaches from a background hatch, strikes a marked center area, then withdraws to reveal a small pit. Impact rules and inclusion remain unapproved.

Wall-Driven Ram

Optional wall-mounted pressure ram strikes one bank and retracts, leaving damaged support. The visible mechanism establishes where the hazard comes from.

Ceiling Strainer Drop

Optional heavy strainer hangs from visible chains, drops onto a warned bank zone, and is hoisted away from the broken floor.

Illustrative camera and hero poses vary. The hand sheet’s lower near/far labels are not a collision diagram; the actual bank axis runs vertically on screen. Use the linked proposal and Level Design plan for geometry and timing.
