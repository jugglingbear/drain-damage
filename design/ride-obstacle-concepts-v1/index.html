Ten ride obstacle proposals

Selection pending. These are concept illustrations, not implemented obstacles or validated jump layouts. Prototype105 and the approved560px/s speed are unchanged.

Ordinary obstacles retain the current1HP policy. Concept10 explicitly proposes a lethal ramp-pit exception. All jump spacing and overhead clearances need validation.

1. Valve-Stem Slalom — Dodge

Alternating bank-connected valve bases close both edge bypasses and require a near/far weave.

Validate full-board-expanded footprints so no straight center or edge bypass remains; spacing and steering time at560px/s remain unvalidated.

2. Elbow-Pipe Breach — Dodge

Pass around a large protruding elbow; the broken pipe core is distinct from its foam.

Keep the opening and spray from obscuring the actual solid contact core.

3. Chain-Hung Plug — Overhead dodge

A suspended rubber plug blocks rider space; its water-plane shadow marks the lane to avoid.

Validate both standing Tony and seated Sean head clearance. Inset costumes are illustrative, not replacement character art.

4. Drain-Brush Curtain — Overhead dodge

Hanging brushes and cables leave a readable open lane. Steer through rather than jump.

Advance silhouettes and shadows must reveal the next curtain before screen entry.

5. Loose Flange Hop — Jump

Hop a low flange ridge with a visible recovery-water stretch beyond.

Size the full ring traversal after jump research; the interior contains water, not a lethal pit.

6. Float-Arm Sweep — Dodge

A hinged cistern float sweeps one lane; a clear bypass avoids its marked sweep zone.

Moving-obstacle timing and guaranteed bypass need separate validation.

7. Cleanout-Cap Islands — Precision jumps

Broad cleanout covers offer whole-board landing decks between unsupported slots.

Target about three board lengths per deck as a study, not a final dimension. Generated profile brackets are illustrative and not measurements.

8. Broken-Basin Hopscotch — Steer + jump

Staggered porcelain slabs combine lane choice with full-board landings and recovery room.

Needs supported-platform mechanics and validated landing/input recovery before chaining hops.

9. Buckled Drain Grate — Jump

Jagged fixed metal spikes protrude through the buckled grate, visibly signaling damaging contact. Jump the complete spike envelope.

Keep spikes distinct from boost ramps; validate board clearance above the tallest tip and safe landing beyond the entire hazard.

10. Emergency Flume Launch — Ramp boost required

A chevron-marked flume launches across an exceptionally long pit into a broad catch basin.

Missing the ramp would cause death: proposed exception to current1HP obstacle policy, requiring explicit selection and validation. Ordinary hops must visibly fall short.
