# Sean and Tony pose sheets

Use the [reviewed lineup](lineup-heads-candidate.png) for relative scale and the portraits in `sources/` for likeness.
Art owns the drawings. Comic reviews the rendered sheets. The opening poses below come from Storyboarding's
review of the [four-page script](../comic-issue-01/opening-script-v2.json).

| Beat | Tony | Sean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Check phone while listening | Walk with loaded service kit and stowed plunger |
| Preparation | Crouch to fit an ankle cover | Fit a cover with equipment set down |
| Conversation | Pause mid-step to question the manager | Listen and try to follow the repair discussion |
| Hazard | Inspect from a dry threshold with a torch | Stop, point toward the hazard and seek Tony's eye |
| Inspection | Explain the repair with an open hand | Recognize the tape roll and exchange a look with Tony |
| Reveal | Startle, then bring nozzle to bear | Startle, then take a steady two-handed plunger grip |
| Guests | Turn and direct the manager toward the guests | Keep sight of the threat as people clear the corridor |
| Door | Secure nozzle and step in beside Sean | Swing door shut, eyes on creature, free arm lowered |
| Impact | Plant a boot and take the load | Lean into the same door and glance toward the breach |

Show both figures together where their relative height, sightlines or shared load tell the story.
For Sean's carrier, cut from the full-length tool stowed cup-up on his back to that same tool in his hand.
Keep the empty holder visible when the angle permits. Keep the attachments and carrier side consistent.

Possible later studies include Tony demonstrating wrench leverage, Sean explaining a control he recognizes,
and the partners passing a tool or supporting a heavy pipe together. These are optional acting references.
