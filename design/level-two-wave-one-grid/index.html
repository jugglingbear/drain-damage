Working scale, not a committed tile system

Neutral flight covers 336 px in 0.6 s; maximum forward travel is 540 px before support tolerance. One 320 px cell leaves only 16 px of neutral-flight travel margin; this is not a generous or universally safe jump. Two full cells (640 px) match tested unjumpable long cuts. Keep exact footprints until Cell-aligned layouts need actual-model validation. A slow approach can make even one cell too long.

Timing and retries

Each strainer warning begins at timeline_origin+1.25 s; impact opens the gap at +3.10 s; its jump volume clears at +3.25 s. Origins use upstream edge: (x − width/2 − 2540)/560 plus seeded ±0.12 s jitter. The overhead grate supplies the warning. The layouts below represent both mirrored arrangements; other seeds also vary timing. Retries retain the seed. No formal checkpoint is designed; legacy retry coordinates in course data are not player checkpoints. Campaign retries start at the bowl; direct course launches restart the slalom.
