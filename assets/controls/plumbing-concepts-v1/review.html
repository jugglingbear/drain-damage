Plumbing controller concepts

Seven mockups — pending selection and Art approval. Images shown at the game’s 315 × 210 display size.

Recommendation: 2, Copper Workshop. Comedy alternative: 6, Salvage Special. Center symbols and final background need production cleanup.

1. White PVC Manifold

Body built from white PVC elbows and a short rectangular central junction box, curved elbow grips wrapped with dark rubber. Orange couplers and blue washer accents.

2. Copper Workshop

Body made from satin copper tubing bent into an original broad open trapezoidal frame around a small dark teal control deck, thick black rubber lower grips, brass compression fittings.

3. Rubber Gasket

Compact broad rounded hexagonal body laminated from chunky black plumbing gaskets and blue rubber layers, oversized steel washers holding a cream central face plate, subtle orange accents.

4. Drain Trap

Original U-shaped silhouette from two chrome sink-trap elbows joining a compact navy junction control deck, dark orange rubber sleeves on lower grips, visible union nuts. Broad balanced proportions.

5. Utility Box

Sturdy small cream rectangular plumbing service box with angled cut-off corners, two short gray PVC side grips sloping downward, teal rubber padding, brass screw heads and orange valve-cap accents.

6. Salvage Special

Lovingly improvised asymmetric but ergonomic controller with olive utility housing, one blue PVC elbow grip and one ribbed black rubber hose grip, mismatched copper brackets, oversized washers and orange repair tape. Intentional practical assembly, not broken.

7. Plumber’s Putty

Soft kneaded putty body with thumb impressions and embedded hardware. Pending approval.
