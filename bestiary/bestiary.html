SPECIMEN 002 / FALSE REASSURANCE

The Hallowed Widow

The first-level revision doubles the flying Widow's width and height and assigns 180 HP. It warns with a faster wingbeat, dives toward the committed hero position, then rises to its pre-dive altitude. It stops for one second, faces the hero’s side, and repeats until killed or the attempt resets. Within six pixels of centered alignment it keeps its heading. Full-body collision sweeps and world bounds apply; a blocked diagonal climb falls back to vertical recovery.

First-level weapons require 15 Tony hits or 10 Sean hits: about 2.2 seconds of accurate firing plus travel. Continuous health bar, hidden by default. Revised loop delivered in Prototype 63 / 0.63.0; packaged checks pass. Live fairness and visual review pending.

Backstory

Backstory. For years, the worst thing about a basement spider was losing sight of it. Then one sprouted wings and apparently started breeding. The Hallowed Widow is a huge, hairy nightmare with ivory fangs, white feathers, and a golden halo—every reassuring detail attached to the wrong animal. Early witnesses watched the floor. The Clogfather

Fatberg identity approved · Boss not implemented

A colossal living fatberg of congealed grease, wet wipes, roots, and things nobody should have flushed. Wedged into the collection chamber beneath headquarters, it heaves against the pipework and spits the accumulated consequences back at anyone approaching.

Provisional design: 720 HP. Continuous boss bar visible by default. No runtime boss HP or encounter yet. 120 pressure bolts or 18 heavy plungers at full damage against the open weak point; misses and closed phases extend combat.

Backstory

Storyboarding draft. The Septic Air Force earned its name when a maintenance crew found a swarm circling a blocked vent with the persistence of aircraft waiting for landing clearance. They have since been spotted throughout the sewers, flying in close formation toward no particular destination. Their buzzing carries impressively far for something with so little to report. Plumbers and monsters alike suffer for wandering into their flight path. Nobody has identified a commanding officer, although several flies appear to be taking credit.

Each creature gets one portrait here, a description, links to its game sprites and animation information, and an honest implementation status. Names and backstories live in each creature’s monster.json. New artwork uses versioned filenames; earlier designs stay preserved.

Game sprites live in assets/sprites/ and assets/bestiary/. Marketing portraits live in bestiary/<creature>/. This gallery and its marketing images are excluded from Godot imports.
