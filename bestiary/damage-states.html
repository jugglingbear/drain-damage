Every hit should register

Persistent damage stages do not replace immediate feedback. Every successful hit should briefly flash the current sprite and show an impact, even between thresholds. Flushfiend has a recoil pose in each living row. The Widow should retain its damaged wingbeat during the flash. Timing and small-scale readability still need engine validation.

Approved direction: ordinary enemy bars hidden by default; optional “Show enemy health bars”; continuous boss bars plus damage visuals; exact HP and damage readouts for development only. These UI changes are not implemented here.
